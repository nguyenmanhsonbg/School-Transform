# Product Vision

Status: DRAFT

## Purpose

Define the product direction for a School Digital Platform serving multiple high schools, without coupling the product vision to a particular implementation technology or customer-specific customization.

## Vision statement

Build a standardized School Core that allows a high school to operate its essential academic and communication workflows on one platform, while supporting tenant-specific configuration, reusable modules, external integrations and controlled customer extensions.

The platform should grow in stages:

1. Establish a reliable foundation.
2. Deliver an end-to-end School Core MVP.
3. Digitize broader school operations.
4. Expand into a Digital School Platform.
5. Add AI capabilities only after the underlying data and workflows are sufficiently mature.

## Product outcomes

- A school can configure its own academic structure and operating rules without changing the core product.
- Multiple schools can use the platform with isolated data and tenant-specific configuration.
- Staff, teachers, students and parents can use the capabilities appropriate to their roles.
- The platform can be demonstrated end-to-end and piloted at one to three partner schools after Phase 1.
- New requirements can be classified as Core, Configuration, Shared Module, Integration or Custom.

## Target context

The initial product context is a Vietnamese high school (THPT). The first release is not intended to be a complete enterprise HRM, accounting, LMS or AI system.

## Product shape

- Product Core contains broadly reusable school capabilities.
- Configuration handles differences that can be represented as rules or settings.
- Shared Modules handle reusable capabilities that are too substantial for configuration alone.
- Integrations connect external systems without changing the School Core unnecessarily.
- Customer Extensions are isolated from the core where a requirement is genuinely customer-specific.

## Non-goals for the initial product

The MVP does not prioritize full HRM, payroll, general accounting, advanced LMS, complete online examination, library, asset management, complex payment, comprehensive admission or broad AI automation.

## Open Questions

- What product scope is required beyond the initial THPT context?
- Which deployment and commercial constraints must influence future product packaging?
- Which pilot-school requirements should become Core versus Configuration or Shared Module?
